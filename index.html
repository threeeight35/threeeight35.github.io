PATIENT-CENTRED COORDINATION

Why Coordinated Medical Travel?

Medical travel requires thoughtful planning, direct communication with treating healthcare professionals, and transparent arrangements.

01

Personalised Coordination

From your initial enquiry through pre-departure planning, you receive dedicated coordination support for the practical parts of your journey.

02

Preliminary Provider Review

Where appropriate and with your authorisation, relevant health information may be provided to independent healthcare professionals in Türkiye for preliminary review. Clinical assessment, suitability and treatment decisions remain entirely with the treating healthcare professionals.

03

Support in Three Languages

Coordination support in English, Arabic and Turkish can assist with practical communication and attendance arrangements around scheduled consultations and clinical appointments.

THE PATIENT JOURNEY

How It Works

From first enquiry to follow-up coordination after you return home.

  1. 1

    Make an enquiry

    Tell us which treatment category you would like to explore and your preferred travel timing. Please do not send medical records, test results, passport or payment details through the website form.

  2. 2

    Provider introductions and quotations

    We connect you with independent healthcare providers and hospitals in Istanbul and arrange consultations and quotations.

  3. 3

    Preliminary provider review, with your authorisation

    Where appropriate, relevant health information may be provided to independent healthcare professionals in Türkiye for preliminary review. This follows a private health-information and overseas-disclosure workflow, not the public web form.

  4. 4

    Receive your written proposal

    Before you proceed you receive your selected coordination package, confirmed inclusions and exclusions, any extras quoted separately and the provider’s treatment quotation.

  5. 5

    Travel and stay coordination

    Your accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul are included in your package, and we coordinate support in English, Arabic and Turkish during your stay.

  6. 6

    Treatment by your independent provider

    Clinical assessment, suitability, informed consent and all treatment decisions, including when you are fit to fly home, remain with the treating healthcare professionals.

  7. 7

    Returning home

    Follow-up coordination continues after you return home. Confirm in advance which records you will bring home and the plan for any concerns once you’re back.

ABOUT

About threeeight35°

threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency is an independent medical travel coordination service for patients from Australia, the UAE and other international markets who are exploring treatment in Türkiye. We manage the practical side of the journey and leave every clinical decision to independent, licensed clinicians.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hommam Azizieh

Founder, threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency

I have been based in Abu Dhabi for more than 15 years and speak English, Arabic and Turkish. I specialise in Marketing & Production with expertise in Photography. Before founding threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency, I had two years of experience working in medical tourism in Istanbul across patient relations and business development.

That experience highlighted how difficult it can be for international patients to navigate treatment information, quotations, travel arrangements and communication across borders. threeeight35° was created to provide a clearer and better-coordinated experience.

I am not a clinician and do not provide medical advice or recommend treatment. My role is to help ensure each patient’s medical travel journey is clearly explained, carefully organised and practically coordinated, while all clinical decisions remain with the treating healthcare professionals.
