Privacy Information

Status: This is a summary of our privacy approach. The full Privacy Policy is being finalised and will replace this page.

What do we collect through the website enquiry form?

Only what is necessary for an initial enquiry: your name, email address, country of residence, preferred contact method, a phone number if you choose WhatsApp, SMS or a phone call as your contact method, the treatment category (and optionally procedure) you are exploring, and an optional general message. We practise data minimisation.

Should I send medical records through the website?

No. We do not ask patients to submit medical records or clinical photographs through public website forms, and the form asks you not to include medical records, test results, passport or payment details.

How is health information handled after my enquiry?

After the initial enquiry, any health information needed for preliminary provider review follows an approved private health-information and overseas-disclosure workflow, with your authorisation. Where appropriate, relevant health information may then be provided to independent healthcare professionals in Türkiye. See Privacy & Overseas Health-Information Disclosure.

Who can access personal and health information?

We handle personal and health information with care and restrict access to authorised personnel.
