Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency (“threeeight35°”, “we”, “us”), the registered business name of ABN 31 329 961 078, collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information, including health information. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Through the website enquiry form we collect only what an initial enquiry needs: your name, email address, country of residence, preferred contact method, a phone number if you choose WhatsApp, SMS or a phone call, the treatment category (and optionally procedure) you are exploring, and an optional message. The form asks you not to include medical records, test results, passport or payment details.

If you go ahead, we may also collect the information needed to coordinate your journey, such as travel dates, passport details for bookings, guest details, and, with your consent, health information that an independent provider needs for a preliminary review.

Health information

Health information is sensitive information. We collect it only with your consent, only when it is needed to coordinate your care, and never through the public website form. It is handled through a separate private process and shared only with the independent healthcare providers you choose to consult. See Privacy & Overseas Health-Information Disclosure.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you by your preferred method.
  • To introduce you to independent healthcare providers and arrange consultations and quotations.
  • To arrange your accommodation, private transfers and in-country support.
  • To prepare your written proposal, invoices and records, and to meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Independent clinics, hospitals and healthcare professionals in Türkiye that you choose to consult, with your consent.
  • Hotels and transport providers, limited to what they need for your bookings.
  • Service providers that help us run the business: our email provider (Google), the services that process website enquiries (Vercel and Resend), our website host (GitHub Pages) and messaging services such as WhatsApp when you choose them.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Overseas disclosure

Because we coordinate treatment in Türkiye and operate desks in Australia and the United Arab Emirates, your information may be disclosed to recipients in Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates. Our service providers may store information in other countries, including the United States. Before health information is sent overseas, we explain who will receive it and ask for your consent.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. Our website host keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Storage, security and retention

We keep personal information in secure accounts, limit access to authorised people and use encrypted connections. Enquiries that do not go ahead are deleted within 2 years. Booking and payment records are kept for 7 years to meet Australian record-keeping requirements, then deleted.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@threeeight35.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its date.
