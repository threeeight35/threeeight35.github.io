Comparing Medical Treatment Quotations

How to compare written treatment quotations and what threeeight35° does and does not price.

What should a written quotation show?

Your complete proposal, including confirmed inclusions and exclusions, is provided in writing before you proceed. It shows your coordination package, any extras quoted separately and the independent provider's treatment quotation.

Why can’t I compare a package price with a treatment price?

They are different things. Treatment is quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital; the threeeight35° package fee covers coordination, accommodation and Istanbul transfers; flights are booked separately. A treatment fee is not combined with a package price into one advertised price.
