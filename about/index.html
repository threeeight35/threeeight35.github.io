About threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency

About Us

threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency is an independent medical travel coordination service for patients from Australia, the UAE and other international markets who are exploring treatment in Türkiye. We manage the practical side of the journey. We connect patients with independent healthcare providers and hospitals in Istanbul, arrange consultations and quotations, and coordinate accommodation, private transfers and English-speaking support during the stay.

Our name reflects how we work. Like a coordinate, we aim to be precise, dependable and clear about where you stand at every step. From the first enquiry with our Australian or UAE desk to follow-up coordination after returning home, patients work with a dedicated coordinator and receive written, itemised proposals before committing.

We are not a healthcare provider. We do not diagnose, prescribe or recommend treatment. Every clinical assessment and all medical care are provided independently by licensed healthcare professionals in Türkiye. Patients are encouraged to discuss overseas treatment with their usual doctor or an appropriate healthcare professional where relevant.

Our Mission

To make international medical travel clearer and better organised for patients and their families through transparent information, personalised coordination and practical support, while respecting privacy and leaving every clinical decision to independent medical professionals.

Our Vision

To be a respected name in international medical travel coordination, known for a standard of transparency and care that lets patients cross borders for treatment with well-informed confidence.

Our Core Values

Patient-Centred Coordination

We listen to each patient’s needs, preferences and circumstances and, where the patient wishes, include companions or family members in appropriate parts of the planning process.

Transparency

We provide itemised written quotations, confirm inclusions in writing and never promise medical outcomes or risk-free procedures.

Privacy and Discretion

We handle personal and health information with care, restrict access to authorised personnel and do not ask patients to submit medical records or clinical photographs through public website forms.

Clear Boundaries

We coordinate. Independent, licensed clinicians make every medical decision.

Precision

We aim for reliable, detail-oriented coordination at every stage of the journey.

Care and Dignity

We treat every patient and companion with attention and respect.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hommam Azizieh

Founder, threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency · Based in Abu Dhabi

Based in Abu Dhabi for more than 15 years, Hommam speaks English, Arabic and Turkish and has two years of experience working in medical tourism in Istanbul across patient relations and business development.

That experience highlighted how difficult it can be for international patients to navigate treatment information, quotations, travel arrangements and communication across borders. threeeight35° was created to provide a clearer and better-coordinated experience.

Hommam is not a clinician and does not provide medical advice or recommend treatment. His role is to help ensure each patient’s medical travel journey is clearly explained, carefully organised and practically coordinated, while all clinical decisions remain with the treating healthcare professionals.
