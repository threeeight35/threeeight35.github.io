AUSTRALIA TO TÜRKİYE

Medical Travel to Türkiye from Australia

A practical guide for Australians researching treatment in Türkiye. We coordinate the journey; independent providers deliver all clinical care.

How does medical travel from Australia to Türkiye work?

Medical travel from Australia to Türkiye generally involves exploring a treatment and independent provider, receiving a written proposal and quotation, arranging travel, attending the provider's assessment, receiving treatment only where the treating clinician considers it suitable, and arranging follow-up after returning to Australia. threeeight35° coordinates the non-clinical journey. See How It Works.

What does threeeight35° coordinate?

We connect patients with independent healthcare providers and hospitals in Istanbul, arrange consultations and quotations, and coordinate accommodation, private transfers and English-speaking support during the stay. Clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, informed consent and medical care are the responsibility of the treating provider.

What should I confirm before travelling?

Confirm the treating clinician, the proposed procedure, anaesthesia and facility arrangements where relevant, expected reviews in Türkiye, records to bring home, and the plan for concerns after returning to Australia.

When can I fly home?

There is no universal safe timeframe. The treating clinician must determine travel timing from the procedure, individual recovery and required post-treatment reviews. See Flying Home After Medical Treatment.

What does it cost, and who do I pay?

Medical treatment and flights are not included in our packages. Medical treatment is quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, and you book your own flights. Each package price includes coordination, accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul for the stay and guests shown. If you prefer to arrange your own accommodation or transport, we will discuss it with you and quote a final package fee. Your complete proposal, including confirmed inclusions and exclusions, is provided in writing before you proceed. See Patient Packages.

What does the Australian Government say?

Smartraveller advises researching the destination health system, making sure travel insurance covers complications and medical evacuation, and notes that basic travel insurance policies rarely cover medical tourism. Read the Smartraveller guidance.
