TAILORED COORDINATION PACKAGES

Medical Travel Coordination Packages

Choose the level of coordination that fits your journey. threeeight35° package prices include our non-clinical coordination, your accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul. Medical treatment and flights are paid separately.

WHO YOU PAY, AND FOR WHAT

Three separate parts of your journey cost

1

Your treatment

Quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital. threeeight35° does not publish, determine or collect medical treatment fees.

2

Your threeeight35° package

Paid to threeeight35°. The fixed package price covers coordination, accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul for the nights and guests shown for your package.

3

Your flights and any extras

You book and pay for your own flights. Extra nights you choose to add are paid directly to the hotel; a longer stay advised by your treating clinician is quoted separately.

PACKAGES

Choose your coordination package

All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD).

Medical treatment and flights are not included in our packages. Medical treatment is quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, and you book your own flights. Each package price includes coordination, accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul for the stay and guests shown. If you prefer to arrange your own accommodation or transport, we will discuss it with you and quote a final package fee. Your complete proposal, including confirmed inclusions and exclusions, is provided in writing before you proceed.
