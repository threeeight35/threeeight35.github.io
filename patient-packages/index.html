TAILORED COORDINATION PACKAGES

Medical Travel Coordination Packages

Choose the level of coordination that fits your journey. threeeight35° package prices cover our non-clinical medical-travel coordination services. Your medical treatment and travel costs are separate.

WHO YOU PAY, AND FOR WHAT

Three separate parts of your journey cost

1

Your treatment

Quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital. threeeight35° does not publish, determine or collect medical treatment fees.

2

Your threeeight35° package

The medical-travel coordination package fee paid to threeeight35°. The package price covers the defined non-clinical coordination services listed for that package.

3

Your travel

Accommodation and private transport are arranged by threeeight35° and itemised separately. They are not represented as included in the coordination package price unless expressly confirmed in writing.
