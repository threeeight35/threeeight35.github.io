Medical Information Disclaimer

What threeeight35° is and is not

threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency is a medical travel and journey coordination service. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or clinical recommendations. Clinical assessments, treatment suitability decisions, medical procedures and clinical oversight are provided independently by appropriately licensed healthcare professionals and healthcare providers in Türkiye.

What this website provides

General information about planning medical travel and coordination. It does not diagnose a condition, recommend a treatment for an individual or replace advice from a suitably qualified healthcare professional. Suitability, risks, alternatives, recovery and fitness to travel must be determined by the treating clinician.

Results and costs

No treatment result, recovery time or travel timeframe is guaranteed. Treatment prices are quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital.

In an emergency

In a medical emergency in Türkiye, call 112. This is separate from threeeight35° contact details.

Discuss your plans

Patients are encouraged to discuss overseas treatment with their usual doctor or an appropriate healthcare professional where relevant.
