Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 22 October 2026

About these terms

These Terms & Conditions apply to the medical travel coordination services provided by threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency (“threeeight35°”, “we”, “us”), the registered business name of ABN 31 329 961 078. Your booking is made when you accept a written proposal from us. If your written proposal says something different from these terms, the written proposal applies.

Our role

threeeight35° is a medical travel coordination service, not a healthcare provider. We do not diagnose, prescribe or recommend treatment. Independent clinics, hospitals and licensed healthcare professionals in Türkiye provide all medical assessment and treatment, and they are responsible for it. We do not guarantee any treatment result, recovery time or travel timeframe.

Packages and what they include

Each coordination package includes medical travel coordination, accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul for the nights and number of guests shown for that package on our Packages page. Your written proposal confirms what is included and excluded for your booking.

Packages do not include medical treatment, clinical fees or international flights. Extra nights you choose to add are paid directly to the hotel. If your treating clinician advises a longer stay, the extra cost is quoted separately. If you prefer to arrange your own accommodation or transport, we will quote a final package fee.

Prices and payment

  • Package prices are in Australian dollars (AUD).
  • A deposit of 50% of the package fee is payable when you accept your written proposal.
  • The balance is payable 14 days before your travel date.
  • Medical treatment is quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, never to threeeight35°.

Cancellations and refunds

The cancellation and refund policy that applies to your booking is set out in, and provided with, your written proposal.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate and complete information, and tell us promptly about any change.
  • Hold a valid passport and any visa or entry documents you need, and book your own flights.
  • Arrange travel insurance. Standard travel insurance rarely covers medical treatment overseas, so check that your policy covers complications and medical evacuation.
  • Follow the advice of your treating clinician, including on when it is safe to travel.

Third-party services

We arrange accommodation and transport with third-party suppliers on your behalf. Their own terms may also apply, and we will tell you about any that affect you.

Liability and your consumer rights

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law, and nothing in these terms limits those rights. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for the medical services of independent healthcare providers, or for events outside our reasonable control, such as flight changes, illness or natural events.

Privacy

We handle your personal information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Complaints, governing law and changes

If you have a concern, email hello@threeeight35.com and we will work with you to resolve it. These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. We may update these terms; the version that applies to your booking is the one in place when you accept your written proposal.
