PATIENT GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our coordination role, package prices, costs and travel from Australia.

Is threeeight35° a healthcare provider?

No. threeeight35° Medical Travel Agency is a medical travel and journey coordination service. Clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, informed consent and medical care are provided independently by licensed healthcare professionals in Türkiye.

Does threeeight35° recommend a treatment or tell me whether I am suitable?

No. We do not diagnose, prescribe or recommend treatment. Suitability is decided by the treating healthcare professional after individual assessment.

How much do your packages cost?

Essential Care is a fixed A$4,950 Medical Travel Coordination Package. Executive Wellness & Recovery is a fixed A$9,900 Medical Travel Coordination Package. VIP Bespoke Health Journey is from A$14,900, with the final coordination package price confirmed in writing after the scope of the journey is established. Every package includes accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul.

Do the package prices include my medical treatment?

No. Medical treatment is quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital. threeeight35° does not publish, determine or collect medical treatment fees.

Are accommodation and private transport included?

Yes. Every package includes accommodation and private transfers in Istanbul between the airport, your hotel and clinic appointments. Essential Care covers 4–7 nights for you and 1 guest (2 people in total), Executive Wellness & Recovery covers 8–10 nights for you and up to 2 guests (3 people in total), and VIP Bespoke Health Journey covers 14 nights for you and up to 5 guests (6 people in total). International flights are not included. Extra nights you choose to add are paid directly to the hotel; a longer stay advised by your treating clinician is quoted separately. For additional guests, please contact us.

Can I arrange my own accommodation or transport?

Yes. Tell us what you would prefer to arrange yourself and we will discuss it with you and quote a final package fee.

Do you publish treatment prices?

No. Treatment prices are quoted by the independent provider after assessment. Your written proposal shows your coordination package, any extras quoted separately and the provider’s treatment quotation before you proceed.

Should I send medical records through the enquiry form?

No. Please do not include medical records, test results, passport or payment details in the website form. After your initial enquiry, any health information needed for preliminary provider review follows a private health-information and overseas-disclosure workflow.

How soon can I fly home after treatment?

There is no universal safe timeframe. The treating clinician determines travel timing from the procedure, your individual recovery and required post-treatment reviews.

What happens after I return to Australia?

Follow-up coordination continues after you return home. Before you travel, confirm which records you will bring home and the plan for any concerns after returning to Australia. Where ongoing care may be needed, discussing your plans with an Australian healthcare professional can help.

Which markets do you serve?

We coordinate medical travel for patients from Australia, the UAE and other international markets. Our Australia desk covers Sydney and Melbourne, our UAE desk covers Dubai and the GCC, and our Istanbul hub provides in-country coordination during your stay.

Is “Türkiye” the same as “Turkey”?

Yes. Türkiye is the country’s name in its own language and is the form we use editorially. Many people still search for Turkey, so you may see both.
