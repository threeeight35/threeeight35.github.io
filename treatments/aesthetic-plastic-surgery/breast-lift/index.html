What to understand before you decide

Before you decide, it helps to understand: individual assessment and planning, the purpose of the proposed procedure, meaningful alternatives and the questions that need individual surgical assessment.

What to confirm before you travel

Confirm the treating clinician, proposed procedure, anaesthesia and facility arrangements where relevant, expected reviews in Türkiye, records to bring home and the plan for concerns after returning to Australia.

When can I fly home to Australia?

There is no universal safe timeframe. The treating clinician must determine travel timing from the procedure, individual recovery and required post-treatment reviews.

How is the cost of treatment quoted?

threeeight35° does not publish treatment prices. Medical treatment and clinical fees are quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, and you should obtain a written provider quotation after assessment. The factors that affect a provider’s quotation are confirmed with the provider for your individual plan.

threeeight35° coordination packages are priced separately — see Patient Packages.

How does threeeight35° fit in?

threeeight35° coordinates communication, travel planning and confirmed journey components around care by independent healthcare providers. Diagnosis, suitability, informed consent, treatment and clinical follow-up decisions belong to the treating healthcare professionals. Read more about threeeight35° and how the journey works.
