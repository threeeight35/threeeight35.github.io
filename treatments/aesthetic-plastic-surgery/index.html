COSTS

How is aesthetic cost handled?

There is no category-wide price. Treatment prices are not published by threeeight35°: medical treatment and clinical fees are quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, in writing, after assessment. threeeight35° coordination package fees are separate — see Patient Packages.
