BARIATRIC & WEIGHT-LOSS SURGERY

Sleeve Gastrectomy in Türkiye

Sleeve Gastrectomy should be researched in the context of sleeve gastrectomy and comparison with gastric bypass. The final clinical plan must be based on individual assessment by the treating healthcare professional.

What to understand before you decide

Before you decide, it helps to understand: sleeve gastrectomy and comparison with gastric bypass, the purpose of the proposed procedure, meaningful alternatives and the questions that need individual surgical assessment.

What to confirm before you travel

Confirm the treating clinician, proposed procedure, anaesthesia and facility arrangements where relevant, expected reviews in Türkiye, records to bring home and the plan for concerns after returning to Australia.

When can I fly home to Australia?

There is no universal safe timeframe. The treating clinician must determine travel timing from the procedure, individual recovery and required post-treatment reviews.

Planning your trip for sleeve gastrectomy

A sleeve gastrectomy pathway includes assessment and tests before surgery, the operation, a hospital stay and checks before you are cleared to fly. The treating team sets each step. Because follow-up continues long after surgery, plan the trip and your care at home together before you book.

What to prepare

  • Your weight history and details of any previous weight-loss treatment.
  • A list of your medical conditions and current medications, shared through the private health-information process.
  • Any recent blood tests or reports your doctor holds.
  • A guest to travel with you. The Executive Wellness & Recovery and VIP packages include accommodation for more than one guest.

Questions to ask the surgical team

  • Which tests are done in Türkiye, and which should be done before you travel?
  • Does the quotation cover the whole pathway: tests, surgery, hospital stay, medications and nutrition support?
  • How many nights in hospital and in Istanbul does the team expect, and who decides when you can fly?
  • Which nutrition and supplement plan will you follow, and for how long?
  • What follow-up does the team provide after you return, and how does it work with your doctor at home?

After you return home

Long-term follow-up, nutrition and supplement monitoring are part of bariatric care. Before you travel, talk to your doctor about who will manage your follow-up at home, and ask the surgical team for a written discharge summary your doctor can use.

How is the cost of treatment quoted?

threeeight35° does not publish treatment prices. Medical treatment and clinical fees are quoted by and paid directly to the independent clinic or hospital, and you should obtain a written provider quotation after assessment. The factors that affect a provider’s quotation are confirmed with the provider for your individual plan.

threeeight35° coordination packages are priced separately — see Patient Packages.

How does threeeight35° fit in?

threeeight35° coordinates communication, travel planning and confirmed journey components around care by independent healthcare providers. Diagnosis, suitability, informed consent, treatment and clinical follow-up decisions belong to the treating healthcare professionals. Read more about threeeight35° and how the journey works.
