DENTAL TREATMENTS

Teeth Whitening in Türkiye

Teeth Whitening should be researched in the context of non-surgical whitening options, limitations and dental assessment. The final clinical plan must be based on individual assessment by the treating healthcare professional.

What to understand before you decide

Before you decide, it helps to understand: non-surgical whitening options, limitations and dental assessment, including whether treatment may require staged care or more than one visit. Treatment sequencing and suitability require dental assessment.

What to confirm before you travel

Confirm the diagnosis or treatment objective, required records or imaging, proposed stages, materials where relevant, temporary or final restorations where relevant, review schedule and what follow-up will be needed after returning to Australia.

What affects the cost?

Cost may vary with the treatment scope, diagnostics, materials, laboratory work, number of teeth or treatment sites and number of stages. The provider supplies the medical-treatment quotation directly. threeeight35° does not publish or collect the treatment fee. The provider quotes in writing after assessment.

threeeight35° coordination packages are priced separately — see Patient Packages.

How does threeeight35° fit in?

threeeight35° coordinates communication, travel planning and confirmed journey components around care by independent healthcare providers. Diagnosis, suitability, informed consent, treatment and clinical follow-up decisions belong to the treating healthcare professionals. Read more about threeeight35° and how the journey works.
